University-Earned Credit

Through Temple University’s eligible actuarial science coursework, I intend to fulfill applicable SOA examination requirements through the University-Earned Credit (UEC) program. Credit is subject to meeting the required academic standards, program eligibility, and official SOA confirmation. Enrollment and course completion do not by themselves grant exam credit.

SOA UEC-eligible exams are FM, SRM, FAM, ALTAM, and ASTAM. Exam P and Exam PA are not UEC-eligible and are planned as SOA examinations. Temple is listed as a Center of Actuarial Excellence with UEC status and states that students may earn credit for those five exams through coursework, subject to the UEC mark and SOA confirmation. Sources: SOA UEC program, SOA Temple University listing, Temple M.S. in Actuarial Science bulletin.
